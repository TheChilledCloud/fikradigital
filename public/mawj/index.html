01 The House

Forty-two rooms, one horizon.

Mawj began in 1952 as a family house on the Latakia corniche, built by a ship captain who wanted his windows to open onto the same sea he crossed for a living. We have kept his habit: every room here faces the water.

The house has been restored slowly, on purpose. Stone, walnut, linen, brass. No two rooms are alike — the building refused to be made uniform, and we stopped trying.

Today, Mawj is a small hotel with forty-two rooms, a private beach, and a kitchen whose menu is set each morning by what the sea brings in.

05 The Coast

Latakia — the old gate of the sea.

Latakia has been a port for four thousand years — first for the Phoenicians, then for everyone who looked east across the Mediterranean. The corniche is still the best seat in the city.

Getting here

Latakia International Airport is twenty minutes from the house. The old city of Latakia is a ten-minute walk along the corniche.

April to October is the season of light; November to March the season of storms — beautiful in its own way.
