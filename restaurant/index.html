Our Story · قصتنا

From the kitchens of the Old City, to your table

What began in 1998 in a tiny kitchen in Bab Tuma — the oldest Christian quarter of Damascus — is today one of Düsseldorf's most loved Arabic tables. Our recipes were written in the margins of grandmother's notebook, in the scent of jasmine evenings and cardamom coffee, and they travelled across borders without losing a single spice.

No shortcuts. No pre-made mixes. Lamb simmered for hours in jameed, rice toasted in ghee, bread baked to order — exactly the way it was done in the alleys of Al-Sham.
